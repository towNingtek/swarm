# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please do not open a public issue for security problems.

Use GitHub's private reporting instead: **Security → Report a vulnerability** on this repository. Include the affected version or commit, the steps to reproduce, and the impact you expect.

We aim to reply within 7 days.

## Scope

In scope:

- the platform: sign-up, invites, accounts, quota, keys and the admin console;
- site isolation: one customer reaching another customer's site, the host, or the platform's model keys;
- the model relay and the site login.

Out of scope:

- what the AI inside a customer's own site can do with that customer's own files. Anyone who can drive the AI on a site can reach everything on that site. That is by design.
